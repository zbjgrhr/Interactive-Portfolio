# Watercolor soundtrack sources

The five recordings in this folder are marked Public Domain or CC0 on Wikimedia Commons. They are bundled here for the interactive portfolio; source records are retained below for provenance.

1. `beethoven-pastoral-scherzo.ogg` — Ludwig van Beethoven, Piano Sonata No. 15 in D major, Op. 28, III. Scherzo; performed by Karine Gilanyan. CC0.  
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Karine_Gilanyan_-_Beethoven_-_Piano_Sonata_nr15_in_D_major_op28_Pastoral_-_III_Scherzo_Allegro_Vivace.ogg
2. `satie-gnossienne-2.ogg` — Erik Satie, Gnossienne No. 2; performed by La Pianista. Public Domain.  
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gnossienne_2_(Satie).ogg
3. `bach-invention-8.ogg` — J. S. Bach, Invention No. 8 in F major, BWV 779. Public Domain.  
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:J.S._Bach%27s_Invention_8_(BWV_779)_on_piano.ogg
4. `schumann-happiness.ogg` — Robert Schumann, Scenes from Childhood, Op. 15, V. Happiness; source performance distributed by Musopen. Public Domain.  
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Robert_Schumann_-_scenes_from_childhood,_op._15_-_v._happiness.ogg
5. `schumann-child-falling-asleep.ogg` — Robert Schumann, Scenes from Childhood, Op. 15, XII. Child Falling Asleep; source performance distributed by Musopen. Public Domain.  
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Robert_Schumann_-_scenes_from_childhood,_op._15_-_xii._child_falling_asleep.ogg
